HOROSCOPE is a pangram: seven letters, every one used.

What do your Spelling Bee games say about you?

Hive Horoscope looks through every NYT Spelling Bee game you've played: the words you found, the order you found them in, and when you played. Then it turns your habits into a tongue-in-cheek horoscope, with the numbers behind it.

  • ☉Sun: the time of day you usually play.
  • ↑Rising: how you start a puzzle.
  • ☾Moon: whether you build on one word or jump around.

You also get charts of your habits, the word you find most and your longest word, a look back at any day's game, and a way to compare with a friend.

    See your own Spelling Bee history

    You copy your history out of the Spelling Bee while you're signed in to nytimes.com, and this page reads it right in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and this page never sees your NYT password.

    With a bookmark

    1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:
      Hive export
    2. Open the Spelling Bee at nytimes.com/puzzles/spelling-bee in your browser and sign in.
    3. Click the Hive export bookmark. A panel in the corner counts through your saved games. It takes about ten minutes; keep that tab open and in front.
    4. When it's done, choose Open my Hive Horoscope. Your results open here.
    On a phone
    2. iPhone or iPad (Safari): bookmark this page, then open Bookmarks, tap Edit, tap the new bookmark, rename it Hive export, and replace its address with the code you copied.
    3. Android (Chrome): bookmark this page with the star in the ⋮ menu, then edit the bookmark: rename it Hive export and replace its URL with the code you copied.
    4. Open nytimes.com/puzzles/spelling-bee in the browser (not the Games app) and sign in. On iPhone, open Bookmarks and tap Hive export. On Android, type Hive export into the address bar and tap the bookmark in the suggestions.
    5. Keep the tab open for about ten minutes, then tap Open my Hive Horoscope.
    Or paste a script into the console
    1. On a computer, open nytimes.com/puzzles/spelling-bee and sign in.
    2. Open the developer tools (Cmd+Opt+I on a Mac, F12 on Windows) and choose the Console tab.
    3. Paste the script below and press Enter. The same panel appears as with the bookmark.
